French Bulldog rescue in Key Biscayne & Miami, Florida

Piper’s Pals Pet Rescue is a Key Biscayne–based rescue serving the Miami, Florida area. Most of our dogs are French Bulldogs — a breed we know and love — but our rescues come in all shapes and sizes, from small breeds to a big-hearted large breed, each looking for a loving forever home. Because French Bulldogs have specific needs and potential health issues, we place those dogs carefully and ask that their adopters have prior experience with the breed or a similar short-nosed breed.
